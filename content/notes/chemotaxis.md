# Chemotaxis

Chemotaxis is the movement of an organism in response to chemical signals. In *E. coli*, receptors detect changes in the environment, signaling networks integrate those changes, and the flagellar motor turns the result into movement.

> [!note] Why it matters to CELLosophy
> The cell does not need to measure a spatial gradient all at once. It compares present conditions with its recent past, making the methylation system a functional analogue of short-term memory.
